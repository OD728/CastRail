# Cast Rail changelog

## 1.0.1

- Minor fixes.
- The classic skin no longer shows a red glow around the cast bar.

## 1.0

First release.

- Two skins for the cast bars: Modern (flat, black edge, soft shadow, icon and cast time) and Classic (the game's frame with a new fill and font).
- Colors: the game's cast colors, class color, Classic bar colors, item quality colors, and the Neon, Cyberpunk, Dracula, Gruvbox, Catppuccin, Rosé Pine, Kanagawa, OneDark and Tokyo Night sets. Interrupted casts turn red and uninterruptible casts grey in every set.
- Bar textures: Flat, two of the game's, five made for this addon, plus LibSharedMedia textures.
- Fonts: the game's four plus LibSharedMedia fonts, with a shadow, outline or thick outline.
- Cast time outside, inside or off; the spell name can be hidden; channels show the spell's name.
- Player, target and focus bars; the player bar keeps its Edit Mode position.
- Options window: `/castrail`, the minimap addon menu, or Options > AddOns > Cast Rail.
- English and German.
